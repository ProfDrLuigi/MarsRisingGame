01 / THE GAME

Defend Deimos.
Take back Mars.

Jump in. Hold your ground. Go again.

Mars Rising is a vertically scrolling arcade shooter created by David Wareing and published by Ambrosia Software. Dodge enemy formations, take out ground targets, and gather the firepower you need to make it through Mars’s landscapes and fortified bases.

01 /

Air & ground

Use cannons against aircraft and rockets against turrets and tanks. When the crosshair turns red, your ground target is in range.

02 /

More firepower

Weapon upgrades, wingmates, shields, and the charged Wave Motion weapon. Collect stars and multipliers to push your high score higher.

03 /

Bring a wingmate

Chase a high score solo or team up at one keyboard. Two ships, separate equipment, and one shared mission.

04 / THE PORT

A piece of Mac history.
Ready for another flight.

This independent port brings Mars Rising to modern macOS. Rebuilt with Swift and SpriteKit, it draws on the familiar maps, artwork, music, and sound effects of the original game.

  • Native macOS app for Apple Silicon and Intel
  • 28 original maps and local two-player co-op
  • Customizable controls, gamepads with rumble, music, sound, and high scores
  • Classic pixel art and optional HD graphics — switch with Ctrl+C
The mission continues.

The port is still being compared with the original. New releases bring fixes and refine gameplay, effects, and timing.

ANY QUESTIONS?

Before you take off.

A Mac running macOS 11.0 Big Sur or later and the published app ZIP. The app includes the game content it needs. Controls are designed for a keyboard; local co-op lets two players share one Mac.

Maps, classic artwork, music, and sound effects come from Mars Rising 1.0.2. The optional HD mode adds new painted artwork based on the original designs. Weapons, enemies, and bonuses are recreated from the original game’s behavior. Comparison work continues, particularly around timing, effects, and less common gameplay situations.

Press Ctrl+C during play or while paused, or choose Game → High-Resolution Graphics in the macOS menu bar. Switching keeps your current run intact, and the app remembers your graphics choice. HD artwork is still in progress, so some graphics use the original artwork in both modes.

The download section points to the latest published release. Download its ZIP and replace your existing app. You can find notes for each version in its GitHub release.
